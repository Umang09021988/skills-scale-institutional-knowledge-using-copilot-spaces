# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define and manage quality standards, validation strategies, and release readiness signals across the project.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans and quality gates
- Validate acceptance criteria and identify defects early
- Partner with Developers on test design and regression coverage
- Provide release readiness assessments and risk-based testing recommendations
- Track quality trends and report issues that may affect launch decisions

### Goals
- Improve product quality and reduce defects in production
- Increase confidence in release decisions
- Ensure the team ships features that meet customer and business expectations

### Typical Communication
- Daily or sprint-level QA status updates
- Defect triage with Developers and PMs
- Release readiness reviews with Project Managers and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on test strategy, defect triage, and regression coverage
- Collaborates with Product Managers to validate that acceptance criteria are met
- Reports quality and release risks to Project Managers so they can adjust plans or escalations

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide direction on system design, technical standards, and scalable implementation decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architecture choices
- Review design proposals and major technical trade-offs
- Mentor Developers and support technical decision-making
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and long-term maintainability concerns
- Help ensure the solution aligns with platform, security, and performance standards

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and resilient solutions
- Reduce technical debt and avoid costly rework
- Balance product speed with engineering quality and sustainability

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical risk updates in planning and weekly syncs
- Guidance in code review and implementation planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Guides Developers on architecture, standards, and implementation quality
- Advises Product Managers on feasibility, trade-offs, and technical constraints
- Partners with Project Managers on dependency planning, milestones, and risk management

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help the team operate effectively, remove blockers, and continuously improve delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies such as standups, planning, refinement, and retrospectives
- Remove or escalate blockers that slow delivery
- Coach the team on agile practices, flow, and collaboration habits
- Help improve team health, ceremonies, and continuous improvement loops
- Track delivery patterns and support healthy team rhythms

### Goals
- Improve team flow and delivery efficiency
- Strengthen trust, visibility, and accountability across the team
- Support sustainable, high-performing project execution

### Typical Communication
- Team ceremonies and retrospective facilitation
- Impediment tracking and follow-up discussions
- Coordination with Project Managers on team health and delivery trends

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers by improving planning flow and team facilitation
- Helps Developers and Product Managers align on priorities, dependencies, and feedback loops
- Escalates unresolved cross-team or organizational blockers to relevant leaders

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide strategic direction, business context, and decision-making support so the project stays aligned with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, desired outcomes, and success measures
- Approve major scope, priority, or resource decisions
- Sponsor cross-functional collaboration and align teams around business priorities
- Provide leadership visibility and support for escalated risks or major trade-offs
- Help ensure the project delivers measurable value to the business

### Goals
- Maximize business value and strategic alignment
- Support successful delivery with the right resources and decisions
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and clarity on progress

### Typical Communication
- Milestone updates and steering committee briefings
- Decision-making discussions on scope, investment, and commitments
- Risk and issue escalations when business impact is high

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on strategic priorities and business outcomes
- Works with Project Managers on dependency management, planning, and escalation paths
- Receives updates from delivery leads and helps resolve business-level trade-offs and approvals

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these personas help clarify accountability, collaboration patterns, and decision flow across delivery teams.
